# Authors

MENHAG is written by (Jimmy) Mohamed Gamal Zaid (University of New Hampshire, Space Science Center, ORCID 0009-0003-3658-3316). The method it implements is derived and validated in the accompanying paper, Zaid, Kole, McConnell, Muleri and Li, "A Generalized Framework for Rapid Polarization Response Generation in Wide Field-of-View Compton Polarimeters", The Astrophysical Journal, 2026, submitted.

## Acknowledgement

The POLAR data and the POLAR responses against which this package was developed and validated were provided by Hancheng Li and Merlin Kole from the POLAR collaboration.
